Green Goblin

Norman Osborn, the Green Goblin is one of Spider‑Man's most dangerous foes — genius, industrialist and menace in a deadly armor.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #14.

Doctor Octopus

Otto Octavius combines brilliant intellect with mechanical tentacles — a persistent, tactical threat who has led the Sinister Six.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #3.

Venom

A symbiotic fusion of alien life and human hosts, Venom is both anti‑hero and terrifying adversary to Peter Parker.

First full appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #300.

Sandman

Flint Marko can transform his body into sand — making him extremely hard to stop and capable of massive destruction.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #4.

Rhino

Aleksei Sytsevich wears a nearly indestructible suit and charges into battle with tremendous force.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #41.

Electro

Max Dillon gains electrical powers after an accident, becoming one of Spider‑Man's shockingly dangerous foes.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #9.

Mysterio

Quentin Beck is a master of illusion and special effects — he uses trickery and spectacle to battle Spider‑Man.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #13.

The Lizard

Dr. Curt Connors transforms into the Lizard after experimenting on himself to regrow a lost arm.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #6.

Vulture

Adrian Toomes uses a harness that grants flight and enhanced strength — an agile and aging adversary.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #2.

Scorpion

Mac Gargan became the Scorpion after being given a powerful mechanical tail and enhanced abilities.

First appearance: The Amazing Spider‑Man #20.
