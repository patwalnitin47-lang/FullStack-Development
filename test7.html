Spider-Man Villains

Meet my Villains

Spider-Man's enemies are more than villains; they reflect his fears, his biggest mistakes, and the darkest sides of power. Every villain brings a different challenge to his life and changes the meaning of heroism.

Green Goblin

The Green Goblin is one of Spider-Man's most personal enemies. With a dangerous mind, brutal weapons, and a deep connection to Peter Parker's life, he is chaos personified and a terrifying symbol of fear.

Doctor Octopus

Doctor Octopus is brilliant, strategic, and almost impossible to outthink. His mechanical arms and scientific genius make him one of the most dangerous minds in the Marvel universe, constantly challenging Spider-Man on every level.

Venom

Venom is a terrifying symbiote that gives its host immense strength, speed, and aggression. He is more than a monster; he is a dark reflection of Spider-Man's own power, making him one of the most feared foes in the entire universe.

Sandman

Sandman can reshape his body into sand, making him unpredictable and highly dangerous. He is a reminder that even villains can carry emotion, sorrow, and conflict, which adds depth to Spider-Man's world.

Mysterio

Mysterio twists reality with illusion and deception, making him one of Spider-Man's most deceptive opponents. He shows that danger does not always come in the form of strength, but often in manipulation, fear, and confusion.
